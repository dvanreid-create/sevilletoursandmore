What you'll do
  1. Travel from Seville to Huelva (1.5 hours)
  2. immerse yourself in the picturesque beauty of the Parque Natural de la Sierra de Aracena y Picos de Aroche, where you can enjoy scenic hiking trails and lush landscapes. (2 hours)
